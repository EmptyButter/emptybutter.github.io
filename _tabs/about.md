I'm an ethical hacker with a background in electronics, embedded systems, firmware, and software development.

I've always enjoyed figuring out how things work, especially when that involves taking them apart, pushing their limits, or making them do something amusingly weird.

These days I spend much of my time exploring offensive security, from web applications and Active Directory to red teaming, Windows internals, and whatever technical rabbit hole catches my attention.

This blog is my personal knowledge base where I document research, experiments, interesting discoveries, and things I've learned along the way. I mainly write for myself, but hopefully you'll find something useful here too.
